✨ opencode ✨

opencode is an open-source AI coding assistant that lives in your terminal. It understands your codebase, writes and edits files, runs commands, and answers questions — all through a natural-language chat interface.

Built to be transparent and extensible, opencode supports custom agents, skills, plugins, and MCP servers, so you can tailor it to your workflow. It works with any LLM provider and keeps your data under your control.

Whether you’re debugging, refactoring, or exploring a new codebase, opencode meets you where you are. It reads your project context, follows your conventions, and gets out of your way when you don’t need it.

Learn more at opencode.ai.
