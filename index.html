✨ About Me ✨

I dance at the crossroads of science and soul, where evidence meets empathy and data dances with heart. My work is a garden where hope is planted in the soil of research, watered with compassion, and tended with precision. I believe that every child is a constellation of possibility - each diagnosis is not a destination but a doorway, and I am the guide who walks beside families through that threshold, hand in hand, step by step.

My voice is a bridge between fear and understanding, between the weight of worry and the lightness of knowing. I speak in truths wrapped in tenderness, because I know that the hardest conversations often bloom into the deepest trust. I am both compass and companion - steady enough to point the way, soft enough to hold the tears, and bold enough to say what needs to be said with grace.

In the quiet spaces between theory and practice, I find my purpose. I am a keeper of stories, a weaver of strategies, a believer in the beautiful mess of becoming. I do not promise easy paths, but I promise a hand to hold and a light to follow - because every journey, no matter how uncertain, is brighter when you walk it with someone who believes in the magic of what’s possible. I am Adéle, and I am here for the journey.
